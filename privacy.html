Overview Privacy Policy Data Requests

Unashamed ("we", "our", "the app") helps people work through compulsive pornography use through self-guided tools, an accountability partner, and small peer communities. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and who can see it.

The short version: we only collect what the app's features actually need to work. We don't run ads, we don't have trackers, and we never sell your data. Anything related to your recovery — your streak, journal, and urge logs — is visible only to you unless you explicitly choose to share it (e.g. with an accountability partner).

Information We Collect

Account informationYour email address and password (your password is never stored in readable form — it's handled by our authentication provider, Supabase). Optionally, a profile photo.
Recovery dataYour streak history, reset logs, urge/panic-button logs, and trigger journal entries.
Accountability dataYour connection to an accountability partner (if you set one up), and any protection status you choose to share with them.
Community dataCircles you join or create, and any posts, comments, or reports you submit. Community participation uses an anonymous handle, not your real name.
Blocking & protection dataApps you flag for blocking and time limits you set. On Android, this list is used on-device to detect when a flagged app is opened; only the fact that it was opened (not what you did inside it) may be logged, and only if you've connected an accountability partner.
Device securityYour PIN and biometric lock are stored securely on your device only — we never receive or store them.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core features: tracking your streak, enforcing blocking you've turned on, and running the accountability and community features.
  • To keep your account secure and let you sign in.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

Who Can See Your Information

  • You always have full access to your own data.
  • An accountability partner you've explicitly connected with can see the specific status you've agreed to share (for example, whether protection is active, or a flagged-app event) — never your journal or private notes.
  • Members of a community circle you've joined can see posts and comments you make in that circle, under your anonymous handle.
  • We (the app's operators) can access data only as needed to run the service, investigate abuse reports, or respond to a legal request.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, a third-party cloud database and authentication provider, and — for images such as profile photos — Bunny.net, a content delivery provider. Both providers encrypt data in transit. Access to your data within our database is restricted by row-level security rules, so, for example, another user cannot query your private journal entries directly.

Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion (see our Data Requests page), we delete your account and associated personal data, except where we're required to retain something for a legitimate legal or safety reason (for example, a moderation report already filed against your account).

Children's Privacy

Unashamed is intended for users aged 17 and older, consistent with the app's Terms & Conditions. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 17.

Your Rights & Choices

You can access, export, or delete your data at any time. See our Data Requests page for how.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

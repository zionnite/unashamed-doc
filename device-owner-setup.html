Scan to set up tamper protection

This QR code sets a child's phone up as Device Owner — the strongest protection tier, where the app can't be Force Stopped or uninstalled without a linked parent's approval.

This completely erases the phone before anything else happens — make absolutely sure it's your child's phone, not yours, before you scan. There's no undo once the reset starts.
  1. Factory reset the phone.Settings → System → Reset options → Erase all data. Everything on it is erased.
  2. At the very first Welcome screen, tap the same spot 6 times in a row.This opens Android's built-in QR provisioning scanner — a standard OS feature.
  3. Connect to WiFi if asked.Needed to download the app.
  4. Scan the QR code above with the phone's camera.
  5. Let it finish — it downloads, verifies, installs, and provisions automatically.
  6. Open Unashamed and sign in as your child's account to confirm protection is active.

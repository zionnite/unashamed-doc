Beyond recovery

Built to help you focus, not just block porn.

The same engine that keeps you off porn works just as well on anything else pulling your attention — social media, games, or just a phone that never stops buzzing.

⏳

App time limits

Pick any app and set a daily minutes cap — once it's hit, that app is done for the day.

🎯

Focus sessions

Start a session and the whole phone goes quiet except calls and texts — stronger than Do Not Disturb, since it can't be casually swiped away.

📅

A schedule that runs itself

Set recurring hours — class, work, deep work — and distracting apps pause automatically, no need to remember to turn anything on.

🌙

Bedtime wind-down

Pick a time and the apps you choose go quiet for the night, so the last thing you do before bed isn't doomscrolling.

Self-managed — no partner or parent required

Every tool here is yours to set up and use on your own, independent of the community or parental oversight side of the app. Calls and texts always stay reachable through a focus session or scheduled block — it's the apps you'd rather put down that pause, not the phone itself.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an accountability partner to use these?

No. App time limits, focus sessions, your schedule, and bedtime wind-down are all self-service from the moment you sign up.

Will I still get calls and texts during a focus session?

Yes — phone and messages stay reachable through every focus session and scheduled block. It's the apps you choose that pause, never your ability to be reached.

Is this a separate app or subscription?

No — it's all part of Unashamed, included with your plan alongside the recovery and community tools.

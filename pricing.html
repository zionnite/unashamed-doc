Pricing FAQ

How much does it cost?

The exact price depends on your region and currency, and is shown to you in the app before your trial converts to a paid subscription — through the App Store or Google Play, the same way any app subscription is priced.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes — 7 days, nothing charged until the trial ends. You can cancel anytime before then with nothing billed.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, directly from your App Store or Google Play subscription settings — the same place you'd cancel any other app subscription. We don't hold a separate billing relationship with you.

What's the difference between Individual and Family?

Individual covers one account. Family covers you plus every child you link through parental oversight, under one subscription instead of billing per child.

Do you accept coupon or redeem codes?

Yes — if you've been given one, enter it during signup for free or discounted access to the relevant plan.

How it works

Linked by invite, nothing hidden

1

You send an invite, they accept

A child links their own account to yours on purpose — there's no silent install or background monitoring mode.

2

Restrictions are visible on both sides

Every parent-set limit shows up clearly as "set by parent" in the child's own app, never disguised as something else.

3

Only one parent link at a time

A child already linked to a parent can't quietly accept a second invite or unlink unilaterally — closing the obvious way around oversight.

What's different between iOS and Android

Android supports the full set of remote controls above. On iOS, parent–child linking and the restrictions Apple's platform exposes to apps are supported; anything Apple keeps private to the device itself is outside what any app — ours included — can remotely see or enforce.

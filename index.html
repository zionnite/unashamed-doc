Questions

Frequently asked

Is Unashamed free?

Signing up includes a free trial. After that, it's a subscription billed through the App Store or Google Play — no separate account or card details needed anywhere else.

Can I just turn blocking off whenever I want?

Not instantly. Blocking is controlled from our servers, not a local toggle, and disabling it takes a real 24-hour waiting period — friction that's there on purpose, for the moment it's hardest to want it off.

Will anyone find out what the app is for?

Notifications are kept generic (e.g. "Check-in reminder"), community features use anonymous handles by default, and a local PIN or biometric lock keeps the app closed to anyone else holding your phone.

What happens if I have a bad day?

You reset and keep going. We don't use words like "failure" or "relapse," and a hard day doesn't erase your progress or get flagged to anyone.

Is the community a public feed?

No. You're matched into a small, moderated circle of 5–10 people, with reporting and moderation built in from day one — not an open timeline.

Is professional coaching or therapy available?

It's on the roadmap as the next layer beyond self-guided tools and peer support. Not available yet — we'll announce it here when it is.

Which platforms does it support?

iOS and Android, both as native apps — not a web app or browser extension.
